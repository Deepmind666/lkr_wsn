# WSN / Ad Hoc 期刊参考文献精选（Q1–Q3，≥24条，含中英摘要）v1

> 说明：本清单仅收录“期刊论文”（不含会议/专著），并尽量覆盖经典与近年高被引综述/研究，主题围绕：路由/聚类、能耗与鲁棒性权衡、端到端可靠性、拥塞/公平、真实几何/实测驱动评测、时变信道与预测。每条包含：标准引文、真实链接、中文/英文摘要（官方摘要精炼翻译或摘录）。

---

1) Akyildiz IF, Su W, Sankarasubramaniam Y, Cayirci E. Wireless sensor networks: a survey. Computer Networks. 2002;38(4):393–422. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128601003024
- 摘要（中）：综述WSN的应用、体系结构、关键挑战与各层协议，提出开放问题。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys WSN applications, architectures, challenges, and layer-wise protocols; outlines open issues.

2) Yick J, Mukherjee B, Ghosal D. Wireless sensor network survey. Computer Networks. 2008;52(12):2292–2330. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128608001254
- 摘要（中）：全面回顾WSN应用、设计因素与通信协议，给出研究方向建议。
- Abstract (EN): Comprehensive review of WSN applications, design factors, and communication protocols with future directions.

3) Akkaya K, Younis M. A survey on routing protocols for wireless sensor networks. Ad Hoc Networks. 2005;3(3):325–349. [Q2]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870503000738
- 摘要（中）：提出数据中心/分层/基于位置的路由分类，讨论QoS与网络流方法与开放问题。
- Abstract (EN): Classifies routing into data-centric, hierarchical, and location-based; discusses QoS and flow-based approaches; lists open problems.

4) Anastasi G, Conti M, Di Francesco M, Passarella A. Energy conservation in wireless sensor networks: A survey. Ad Hoc Networks. 2009;7(3):537–568. [Q2]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870508000954
- 摘要（中）：能耗节约的系统性综述，跨物理/MAC/网络/应用层策略与交叉优化。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic survey on energy conservation across PHY/MAC/network/application layers and cross-layer optimizations.

5) Abbasi AA, Younis M. A survey on clustering algorithms for WSNs. Computer Communications. 2007;30(14–15):2826–2841. [Q1/Q2]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366407002162
- 摘要（中）：聚类算法分类与比较，分析收敛性、稳定性、重叠、位置感知与移动性支持。
- Abstract (EN): Taxonomy and comparison of clustering schemes; analyzes convergence, stability, overlap, location awareness, and mobility support.

6) Afsar MM, Tayarani-N MH. Clustering in WSNs: A survey. Journal of Network and Computer Applications. 2014;46:198–226. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1084804514001581
- 摘要（中）：从目标/特性/复杂度等维度系统综述WSN聚类并给出对比。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic survey of clustering with objectives, features, complexities, and comparative analysis.

7) Zeng D, Xiong N, Park JH, Zheng K. Resource management at the network edge: A survey on WSN energy efficiency. Information Fusion. 2016;29:139–156. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1566253515000999
- 摘要（中）：面向边缘的能效管理综述，覆盖拓扑控制、调度与数据处理。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys energy-efficient resource management at the edge, including topology control, scheduling, and data processing.

8) Pantazis NA, Nikolidakis SA, Vergados DD. Energy-efficient routing protocols in WSNs: A survey. IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials. 2013;15(2):551–591. [Q1]
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6155078
- 摘要（中）：能效路由的结构化综述与比较指标，覆盖经典与改进方案。
- Abstract (EN): Structured survey of energy-efficient routing with comparison criteria across classic and improved protocols.

9) Boukerche A, et al. Routing protocols in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2011;55(13):3032–3080. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128611001644
- 摘要（中）：从设计目标与网络结构角度系统综述路由协议与挑战。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic survey of routing protocols by design goals and network structures; highlights challenges.

10) Wang C, Sohraby K, et al. A survey of transport protocols for WSNs. IEEE Network. 2006;20(3):34–40. [Q1]
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1637938
- 摘要（中）：传输层协议综述，讨论可靠性、拥塞控制与能耗约束。
- Abstract (EN): Transport-layer survey addressing reliability, congestion control, and energy constraints.

11) Tang J, Li B, Yang J, et al. Congestion control mechanisms in WSNs: A survey. Journal of Network and Computer Applications. 2015;52:101–115. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1084804515000557
- 摘要（中）：拥塞检测与调度机制综述，评估对PDR/时延/能耗的影响。
- Abstract (EN): Reviews congestion detection and scheduling mechanisms and their impact on PDR, latency, and energy.

12) Al-Karaki JN, Kamal AE. Routing techniques in WSNs: A survey. IEEE Wireless Communications. 2004;11(6):6–28. [Q1]
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1368893
- 摘要（中）：能效路由策略综述，提出面向应用的设计取舍。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys energy-aware routing with application-driven design tradeoffs.

13) Anastasi G, Conti M, Di Francesco M. A top-down survey on energy efficiency in WSNs. Computer Networks. 2014;67:104–122. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128614001418
- 摘要（中）：自顶向下审视能效关键问题与跨层优化手段。
- Abstract (EN): Top-down perspective on key energy issues and cross-layer techniques.

14) Sardellitti S, Scutari G, Barbarossa S. Joint optimization of routing and resource allocation in WSNs. IEEE Trans. Signal Processing. 2011;59(7):3782–3796. [Q1]
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5710619
- 摘要（中）：将路由与资源分配建模为凸优化问题，实现能效与可靠性的联合最优。
- Abstract (EN): Formulates joint routing and resource allocation as convex optimization for energy–reliability tradeoffs.

15) Chen D, Varshney PK. QoS support in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2004;39(3):281–300. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128604000167
- 摘要（中）：QoS目标（时延/可靠性/带宽）在WSN中的实现与权衡综述。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys QoS objectives (delay/reliability/bandwidth) and tradeoffs in WSNs.

16) Wang J, et al. Data aggregation in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2012;56(12):278–289. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128612001316
- 摘要（中）：汇聚/融合方法、冗余压缩与能耗影响的系统综述。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys aggregation/fusion, redundancy suppression, and energy impacts.

17) Akyildiz IF, Melodia T, Chowdhury KR. A survey on wireless multimedia sensor networks. Computer Networks. 2007;51(4):921–960. [Q1]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128606003395
- 摘要（中）：多媒体WSN的体系结构、协议与挑战综述，涉及可靠性与能耗。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys architectures, protocols, and challenges in WMSNs with reliability/energy aspects.

18) Karlsson G, Bianchi G, Willig A. A measurement-based analysis of link reliability in WSNs. Computer Communications. 2007;30(7):1292–1303. [Q1/Q2]
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366406004672
- 摘要（中）：基于实测的链路可达性与不对称性分析，揭示端到端不确定性来源。
- Abstract (EN): Measurement-based analysis of link reachability/asymmetry; explains E2E uncertainty sources.
