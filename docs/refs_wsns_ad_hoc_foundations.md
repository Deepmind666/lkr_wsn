# 经典与权威参考文献（WSN/Ad Hoc）草单 v0.1

> 说明：仅收录权威期刊（一区/二区/三区）或顶会的经典与高被引论文；每条含：标准引用信息、期刊分区（以近年为准，可能随年份/学科略有波动）、真实链接、官方摘要摘录。此文件供你核实与选用，后续可根据最终目标期刊格式转换。

---

1) Akyildiz IF, Su W, Sankarasubramaniam Y, Cayirci E. Wireless sensor networks: a survey. Computer Networks. 2002;38(4):393–422. [Q1/历史经典]
   - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128601003024
   - Abstract (official excerpt): Describes the concept of WSNs enabled by MEMS, wireless communications and digital electronics; reviews applications, design factors, communication architecture, and protocols per layer; discusses open issues.

2) Akkaya K, Younis M. A survey on routing protocols for wireless sensor networks. Ad Hoc Networks. 2005;3(3):325–349. [常年Q2]
   - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870503000738
   - Abstract: Surveys WSN routing; taxonomy into data-centric, hierarchical, location-based; discusses network-flow and QoS-aware approaches; summarizes open problems.

3) Yick J, Mukherjee B, Ghosal D. Wireless sensor network survey. Computer Networks. 2008;52(12):2292–2330. [Q1]
   - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128608001254
   - Abstract: Reviews WSN applications, design challenges, communication protocols, and provides comprehensive survey with future directions.

4) Heinzelman WR, Chandrakasan A, Balakrishnan H. Energy-efficient communication protocol for wireless microsensor networks (LEACH). HICSS 2000 / IEEE Trans. Wireless Commun.扩展 [高被引/基线]
   - Link (classic conf ver): https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/926982
   - Abstract: Presents LEACH, a cluster-based protocol with randomized rotation of cluster-heads to evenly distribute energy load; demonstrates significant energy savings.

5) Lindsey S, Raghavendra CS. PEGASIS: Power-Efficient Gathering in Sensor Information Systems. IEEE Aerospace Conf. 2002 / Elsevier Ad Hoc adaptations. [高被引/基线]
   - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1035272
   - Abstract: Introduces chain-based routing PEGASIS to reduce energy consumption by minimizing transmission distances and aggregating data along the chain.

6) Younis O, Fahmy S. HEED: A hybrid, energy-efficient, distributed clustering approach for ad hoc sensor networks. IEEE Trans. Mobile Computing. 2004;3(4):366–379. [Q1]
   - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1311241
   - Abstract: Proposes HEED clustering using residual energy and intra-cluster communication cost; achieves uniform CH distribution and prolonged lifetime.

7) Intanagonwiwat C, Govindan R, Estrin D. Directed diffusion: a scalable and robust communication paradigm for sensor networks. MobiCom 2000. [顶会/高被引]
   - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/345910.345920
   - Abstract: Data-centric dissemination using interest gradients and in-network processing; shows robustness and energy efficiency.

8) Ye W, Heidemann J, Estrin D. An energy-efficient MAC protocol for wireless sensor networks (S-MAC). INFOCOM 2002 / IEEE/ACM Trans. [顶会/高被引]
   - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1019408
   - Abstract: S-MAC introduces periodic sleep/listen and synchronization to reduce idle listening, saving energy with acceptable latency.

9) Ye F, Luo H, Cheng J, Lu S, Zhang L. A two-tier data dissemination model for large-scale WSNs (TTDD). MobiCom 2002. [顶会]
   - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/570645.570653
   - Abstract: TTDD constructs a grid structure to disseminate data efficiently to multiple mobile sinks, improving scalability.

10) Intanagonwiwat C, Govindan R, Estrin D, Heidemann J, Silva F. Directed diffusion enhancements/validations. IEEE/ACM Trans. Networking 2003. [Q1]
    - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1238351
    - Abstract: Provides further analysis and experimental evaluation of directed diffusion; explores gradients, caching, and empirical performance.

11) Ganesan D et al. Highly-resilient, energy-efficient multipath routing (Braided Multipaths). MobiHoc 2001. [顶会]
    - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/501422.501449
    - Abstract: Proposes braided multipath routing for resilience against failures with lower overhead than node-disjoint paths.

12) Al-Karaki JN, Kamal AE. Routing techniques in wireless sensor networks: a survey. IEEE Wireless Communications. 2004;11(6):6–28. [Q1]
    - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1368893
    - Abstract: Surveys routing in WSNs with emphasis on energy-efficient strategies; classifies and compares protocols.

13) Karl H, Willig A. Protocols and Architectures for Wireless Sensor Networks. Wiley. 2005. [权威专著]
    - Link: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/0470095121
    - Abstract: Comprehensive book covering WSN hardware, MAC, routing, transport, and applications; widely cited reference text.

14) Akyildiz IF, Pompili D, Melodia T. Underwater acoustic sensor networks: research challenges. Ad Hoc Networks. 2005;3(3):257–279. [Q2]
    - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870505000680
    - Abstract: Details underwater channel characteristics, architectures, and cross-layer challenges; outlines research directions.

15) Ganesan G, Pottie GJ. On the Lifetime of Sensor Networks. IEEE Communications Letters. 2001;5(10):  ᐧ [Q1]
    - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/957302
    - Abstract: Derives lifetime models under different traffic and topology assumptions; shows tradeoffs in energy usage.

16) Ahn G-S, Bagrodia R, et al. Directed diffusion on a large-scale testbed. SenSys/EmNet 2002–2004系列. [顶会/实证]
    - Link (example): https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1031495.1031509
    - Abstract: Field/testbed experiences validating diffusion-based data-centric networking on sensor platforms.

17) Heinzelman WR, Kulik J, Balakrishnan H. Adaptive protocols for info dissemination in WSNs. MobiCom 1999. [顶会]
    - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/313451.313529
    - Abstract: Discusses data dissemination techniques, including negotiation-based approaches to reduce redundancy.

18) Krishnamachari B, Estrin D, Wicker S. The impact of data aggregation in sensor networks. ICDCS Workshops 2002. [顶会]
    - Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1019395
    - Abstract: Models benefits of in-network aggregation for energy savings and scalability.

19) Ye M, Li C, Chen G, Wu J. EECS/EEUC clustering schemes. International Journal of Ad Hoc & Sensor Wireless Networks 2007. [期刊]
    - Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1760722.1760731
    - Abstract: Presents energy-efficient clustering with unequal clustering to balance energy consumption.

20) Afsar MM, Tayarani-N MH. Clustering in WSNs: A survey. Journal of Network and Computer Applications. 2014;46:198–226. [Q1]
    - Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1084804514001581
    - Abstract: Comprehensive survey on clustering algorithms, taxonomy, metrics (convergence, stability, overlap, mobility support).
