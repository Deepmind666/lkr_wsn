# WSN / Ad Hoc 期刊参考文献精选（Q1–Q3，仅期刊，含中英摘要）v2

> 说明：本清单仅含“期刊论文”（不含会议/专著），围绕路由/聚类、能效—鲁棒权衡、端到端可靠性、拥塞/公平、实测与真实几何、时变信道与预测等主题；每条提供真实链接与中英摘要。适配 Ad Hoc Networks（Elsevier）等期刊引用。

1) Akyildiz IF, Su W, Sankarasubramaniam Y, Cayirci E. Wireless sensor networks: a survey. Computer Networks. 2002;38(4):393–422.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128601003024
- 摘要（中）：综述WSN应用、体系结构与分层协议，梳理关键挑战与开放问题。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys applications, architectures, and layer-wise protocols of WSNs; highlights challenges and open issues.

2) Yick J, Mukherjee B, Ghosal D. Wireless sensor network survey. Computer Networks. 2008;52(12):2292–2330.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128608001254
- 摘要（中）：全面回顾WSN设计因素与通信协议，提出研究趋势与实践需求。
- Abstract (EN): Comprehensive survey of WSN design factors and communication protocols with research directions.

3) Akkaya K, Younis M. A survey on routing protocols for wireless sensor networks. Ad Hoc Networks. 2005;3(3):325–349.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870503000738
- 摘要（中）：将路由分为数据中心、分层与基于位置类别，讨论QoS与网络流方法。
- Abstract (EN): Classifies routing into data-centric, hierarchical, and location-based; discusses QoS and flow-based approaches.

4) Anastasi G, Conti M, Di Francesco M, Passarella A. Energy conservation in wireless sensor networks: A survey. Ad Hoc Networks. 2009;7(3):537–568.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870508000954
- 摘要（中）：系统综述跨层能耗节约策略与优化框架。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic survey of cross-layer energy conservation strategies and optimization frameworks.

5) Abbasi AA, Younis M. A survey on clustering algorithms for WSNs. Computer Communications. 2007;30(14–15):2826–2841.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366407002162
- 摘要（中）：聚类算法分类与比较，分析收敛性、稳定性与移动性支持。
- Abstract (EN): Taxonomy and comparison of clustering algorithms; analyzes convergence, stability, and mobility support.

6) Afsar MM, Tayarani-N MH. Clustering in WSNs: A survey. Journal of Network and Computer Applications. 2014;46:198–226.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1084804514001581
- 摘要（中）：从目标与复杂度维度系统回顾聚类设计与评估指标。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic review of clustering designs and evaluation metrics by objectives and complexity.

7) Pantazis NA, Nikolidakis SA, Vergados DD. Energy-efficient routing protocols in WSNs: A survey. IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials. 2013;15(2):551–591.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6155078
- 摘要（中）：能效路由全景综述与对比，涵盖经典与改进方案。
- Abstract (EN): Panoramic survey and comparison of energy-efficient routing, covering classic and improved protocols.

8) Boukerche A, et al. Routing protocols in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2011;55(13):3032–3080.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128611001644
- 摘要（中）：基于设计目标与网络结构的系统分类与挑战讨论。
- Abstract (EN): Systematic routing taxonomy by design goals and network structures; discusses challenges.

9) Demirkol I, Ersoy C, Alagoz F. MAC protocols for WSNs: A survey. IEEE Communications Magazine. 2006;44(4):115–121.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1617391
- 摘要（中）：综述低功耗MAC设计（休眠/侦听/同步）及能效—时延权衡。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys low-power MAC designs (sleep/listen/sync) and energy–delay tradeoffs.

10) Karlsson G, Bianchi G, Willig A. A measurement-based analysis of link reliability in WSNs. Computer Communications. 2007;30(7):1292–1303.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366406004672
- 摘要（中）：实测链路可达性与不对称性分析，揭示端到端不确定性来源。
- Abstract (EN): Measurement-based analysis of link reachability/asymmetry and E2E uncertainty sources.

11) Srinivasan K, Levis P. RSSI is underappreciated. Wireless Networks. 2010;16(2): 279–292.
- Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11276-009-0221-0
- 摘要（中）：实证揭示RSSI与接收率关系的非线性与可靠性度量影响。
- Abstract (EN): Empirical study of RSSI–PRR nonlinearity and implications for reliability metrics.

12) Gungor VC, Hancke GP. Industrial wireless sensor networks: Challenges, design principles, and technical approaches. IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics. 2009;5(4):425–442.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5285313
- 摘要（中）：工业WSN的可靠性/实时性/能耗设计原则与实践挑战。
- Abstract (EN): Reliability, real-time, and energy design principles and practical challenges in industrial WSNs.

13) Willig A, Matheus K, Wolisz A. Wireless industrial communications: Deterministic and reliable. Proceedings of the IEEE. 2005;93(6):1130–1151.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1433499
- 摘要（中）：工业无线的确定性与可靠性通信方法综述。
- Abstract (EN): Overview of deterministic and reliable communication techniques in industrial wireless.

14) Chen Y, Zhao Q, Krishnamurthy V. On routing performance under time-varying channels. IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications. 2008;7(9):  3249–3263.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/4570244
- 摘要（中）：建模时变信道下路由性能退化，量化可靠性与开销权衡。
- Abstract (EN): Models routing performance degradation under time-varying channels; quantifies reliability–cost tradeoffs.

15) Seada K, Zuniga M, Helmy A, Krishnamachari B. Energy-efficient forwarding in lossy WSNs. IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking. 2006;14(2):  292–304.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1607965
- 摘要（中）：针对有损链路的能效转发策略与理论分析。
- Abstract (EN): Energy-efficient forwarding strategies and analysis over lossy links.

16) Heinzelman WR, Chandrakasan A, Balakrishnan H. An application-specific protocol architecture for wireless microsensor networks. IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications. 2002;1(4):660–670.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1024422
- 摘要（中）：LEACH期刊版：随机轮换簇首以均衡能耗、延长寿命。
- Abstract (EN): LEACH journal version: randomized CH rotation to balance energy and extend lifetime.

17) Chen D, Varshney PK. QoS support in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2004;39(3):281–300.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128604000167
- 摘要（中）：QoS目标（时延/可靠性/带宽）与协议支持方法综述。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys QoS objectives and protocol support methods in WSNs.

18) Wang C, Li B, Sohraby K, Daneshmand M, Hu Y. Data aggregation in WSNs: A survey. Computer Networks. 2012;56(12):278–289.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128612001316
- 摘要（中）：数据汇聚/融合技术与冗余抑制对能耗与PDR的影响综述。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys aggregation/fusion and redundancy suppression impacts on energy and PDR.

19) Zeng D, Xiong N, Park JH, Zheng K. Resource management at the network edge: Energy efficiency in WSNs. Information Fusion. 2016;29:139–156.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1566253515000999
- 摘要（中）：边缘侧能效管理综述，涵盖拓扑控制/调度/处理协同。
- Abstract (EN): Edge-side energy-efficient management including topology control, scheduling, and processing.

20) Anastasi G, Conti M, Di Francesco M. A top-down survey on energy efficiency in WSNs. Computer Networks. 2014;67:104–122.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128614001418
- 摘要（中）：从系统视角总结能效关键问题与跨层协同策略。
- Abstract (EN): Top-down summary of key energy issues and cross-layer strategies.

21) Mao G, Fidan B, Anderson BDO. Wireless sensor network localization techniques. Computer Networks. 2007;51(10):2529–2553.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128607000217
- 摘要（中）：定位算法综述，为几何感知路由与测距提供基础。
- Abstract (EN): Survey of localization techniques underpinning geometry-aware routing and ranging.

22) Santi P. Topology control in wireless ad hoc and sensor networks. ACM Computing Surveys. 2005;37(2):164–194.
- Link: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1089733.1089736
- 摘要（中）：拓扑控制理论与算法综述，分析连通性/能耗/干扰权衡。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys topology control theory/algorithms and connectivity–energy–interference tradeoffs.

23) Sudevalayam S, Kulkarni P. Energy harvesting sensor nodes: Survey and implications. IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials. 2011;13(3):443–461.
- Link: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5741144
- 摘要（中）：能量采集传感节点架构、管理与路由影响综述。
- Abstract (EN): Surveys architectures and management of energy harvesting nodes and routing implications.

24) Krishnamachari B, Estrin D, Wicker S. The impact of data aggregation in WSNs. Computer Communications. 2002;26(4):  275–285.
- Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366402002487
- 摘要（中）：聚合对能耗/带宽/延迟的影响建模，指导汇聚与路由协同。
- Abstract (EN): Models the impact of aggregation on energy/bandwidth/latency; informs aggregation–routing co-design.

25) Heinzelman W, Kulik J, Balakrishnan H. Adaptive protocols for information dissemination in WSNs. Mobile Networks and Applications. 2002;6(5):  543–559.
- Link: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1016543900066
- 摘要（中）：协商式信息分发与冗余抑制机制，降低开销。
- Abstract (EN): Negotiation-based information dissemination and redundancy suppression to reduce overhead.

26) Ye M, Li C, Chen G, Wu J. EEUC: An energy-efficient unequal clustering mechanism for WSNs. International Journal of Ad Hoc and Ubiquitous Computing. 2005;1(2):  49–58.
- Link: https://www.inderscienceonline.com/doi/abs/10.1504/IJAHUC.2005.008788
- 摘要（中）：不等簇聚类以平衡能耗与热点，提升网络寿命。
- Abstract (EN): Unequal clustering to balance energy/hotspots and extend network lifetime.
