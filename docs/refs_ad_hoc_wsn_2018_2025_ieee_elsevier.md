1) Shahraki A, Taherkordi A, Haugen Ø, Eliassen F. Clustering objectives in wireless sensor networks: A survey and research direction analysis. Computer Networks. 2020;180:107376. doi:10.1016/j.comnet.2020.107376
- 链接（Link）：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1389128620303121
- 英文摘要（官方精炼）：Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) comprise resource-constrained nodes and face management challenges at scale (resource, scalability, reliability). Clustering is a key topology-management method. This survey refines >500 techniques, reviews ~215 in depth, and classifies them by objectives (beyond energy) and network properties (mobility, heterogeneity). Statistics across metrics provide insights for the design of clustering in WSNs.
- 中文摘要（精译）：无线传感器网络（WSN）由受资源约束的节点构成，在大规模部署下面临资源管理、可扩展性与可靠性等管理挑战。聚类是典型的拓扑管理手段。本综述在筛选 500 余项技术的基础上，深入评估约 215 项，并按“目标（不仅限于能耗）”与“网络属性（如移动性、异构性）”进行分类与分析；同时提供基于指标的统计结果，为聚类设计提供可操作的参考与洞见。
- 建议引用格式（近似 Ad Hoc Networks 样式）：Shahraki A, Taherkordi A, Haugen Ø, Eliassen F. Clustering objectives in wireless sensor networks: A survey and research direction analysis. Computer Networks. 2020;180:107376. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2020.107376

2) Temene N, Sergiou C, Georgiou C, Vassiliou V. A Survey on Mobility in Wireless Sensor Networks. Ad Hoc Networks. 2022;125:102726. doi:10.1016/j.adhoc.2021.102726
- 链接（Link）：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570870521002158
- 英文摘要（官方精炼）：Reviews mobility (mobile sinks/nodes) to address congestion, hotspots, and lifetime; classifies algorithms; discusses IoT adaptation and open problems.
- 中文摘要（精译）：综述 WSN 移动性（移动汇聚/移动节点）以缓解拥塞和热点、延长寿命；系统分类算法，并讨论面向 IoT 的适配与开放问题。
- 建议引用格式：Temene N, Sergiou C, Georgiou C, Vassiliou V. A Survey on Mobility in Wireless Sensor Networks. Ad Hoc Networks. 2022;125:102726. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adhoc.2021.102726

3) Thomson C, Fouda MME, O’Farrell T, Al-Dubai A. Towards an energy balancing solution for wireless sensor network with mobile sink node. Computer Communications. 2021;170:50–64. doi:10.1016/j.comcom.2021.01.016
- 链接（Link）：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0140366421000323
- 英文摘要（官方精炼）：Proposes energy balancing via a mobile sink to mitigate hotspots and extend lifetime; quantifies delay/throughput tradeoffs; practical guidelines.
- 中文摘要（精译）：提出利用移动汇聚实现能量均衡，缓解热点并延长网络寿命；量化时延/吞吐权衡并给出工程建议。
- 建议引用格式：Thomson C, Fouda MME, O’Farrell T, Al-Dubai A. Towards an energy balancing solution for wireless sensor network with mobile sink node. Computer Communications. 2021;170:50–64. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comcom.2021.01.016

4) Yue Y-G, He P. A comprehensive survey on the reliability of mobile wireless sensor networks: Taxonomy, challenges, and future directions. Information Fusion. 2018;44:188–204. doi:10.1016/j.inffus.2018.03.005
- 链接（Link）：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1566253517307777
- 英文摘要（官方精炼）：Comprehensive review of reliability in mobile WSNs, covering taxonomy, methods for reliable data collection and transmission, challenges, and future directions.
- 中文摘要（精译）：系统综述移动WSN的可靠性研究，涵盖分类体系、数据采集与传输的可靠方法、关键挑战与未来方向。
- 建议引用格式：Yue Y-G, He P. A comprehensive survey on the reliability of mobile wireless sensor networks: Taxonomy, challenges, and future directions. Information Fusion. 2018;44:188–204. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2018.03.005

5) Nguyen L, Nguyen HT. Mobility based network lifetime in wireless sensor networks: A review. Computer Networks. 2020;174:107236. doi:10.1016/j.comnet.2020.107236
- 链接（Link）：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1389128619315865
- 英文摘要（官方精炼）：Surveys mobility-driven techniques (mobile nodes/sinks) to maximize WSN lifetime; classifies strategies and discusses energy-efficiency insights and research directions.
- 中文摘要（精译）：综述通过移动节点/移动汇聚提升 WSN 寿命的方法；系统分类并总结能效洞见与研究方向。
- 建议引用格式：Nguyen L, Nguyen HT. Mobility based network lifetime in wireless sensor networks: A review. Computer Networks. 2020;174:107236. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2020.107236
